# Data Processing Agreement — school-facing template (round-2 LEG-1 groundwork)

Status: **template for operator review** — not legal advice; have counsel
review before signing with any school. Kept in-repo so the classroom stack
(EDU-1..8) can point schools at a concrete artifact instead of a promise.

## Parties

- **Processor**: WebCoder (the platform operator, henceforth "the Platform")
- **Controller**: ____________________ (the school / county school inspectorate,
  henceforth "the School")

The School acts as controller for its students' personal data processed
through classroom features (classes, teme, quiz answers, uploaded
deliverables, grade exports). The Platform acts as processor on the
School's documented instructions (teacher-created classes and assignments).

## 1. Subject matter and duration

Processing covers: student accounts enrolled by self-join into
School-operated classes, their submissions and auto-graded results, quiz
answer sheets, uploaded assignment files, and the CSV gradebook exports
generated by teachers. Duration: the school year plus any retention period
in §4.

## 2. Categories of data subjects and data

- Students of the School (minors included — GDPR Art. 8 age in Romania is
  16; below 16 enrollment is school-mediated under the School's lawful
  basis, never self-serve consent).
- Teachers of the School (accounts, class rosters, grade exports).
- Data: usernames, (optionally) county/school fields, email addresses of
  teachers, submission metadata and source code, quiz answers, uploaded
  files, grades. No special-category data is processed; the Platform does
  not require real names of students.

## 3. Processor obligations

The Platform shall: process only on the School's instructions; keep the
data within the EEA; apply the technical measures documented in the
Platform's security documentation (sandboxed judging, encrypted transport,
httpOnly-cookie sessions, rate limiting); notify the School of any
personal-data breach within 72 hours; assist with data-subject requests
(export/purge endpoints under /users/me/export/); delete or return all
class-scoped data on termination within 90 days.

## 4. Sub-processors

Docker/hosting vendor(s) used to run the Platform. The School will be
informed of any new sub-processor 30 days before it takes effect.

## 5. Minors' defaults

Minors' profiles default non-public; county/school identity fields never
render on their public profiles; exam-mode lockdown (EDU-4) restricts
community surfaces during exams.
